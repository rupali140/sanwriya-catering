    How booking works

    From enquiry to event day.

    1. Request a quote

      Tell us the event, date, guest count and the dishes you like.

    2. We talk it through

      We discuss your menu and requirements and settle the details with you.

    3. You get a quotation

      A clear quotation built around your menu, guests and services.

    4. Pay the advance

      The advance confirms your booking once we have verified it.

    5. We cook and serve

      Our team prepares, sets up and serves at your venue.

    6. Settle the balance

      The remaining payment is settled as agreed in your quotation.

    Please note: the advance payment is non-refundable.
